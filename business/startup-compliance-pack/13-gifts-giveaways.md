# 13 — GIFTS & WEEKLY GIVEAWAYS

**Status:** DRAFT · **Risk:** MEDIUM (ASA/Gambling Act/tax) · **Approval:** L3 per promotion

## 1. Legal shape of the weekly giveaway

A prize draw with a **free entry route** is not a licensable lottery; a paid-entry draw without a skill element can be an **illegal lottery**. Rules for every No Games weekly giveaway:
- [ ] Free to enter (e.g. follow/tag/newsletter signup) OR a genuine free-entry alternative that is equally accessible and prominently stated.
- [ ] If purchase-linked ("every order this week is entered"), a free postal/online entry route must exist and be publicised.
- [ ] No skill-free paid lotteries. If in doubt → free-entry route. Ambiguous formats: **L5 check before launch.**

## 2. CAP Code / ASA requirements (every promotion)

Published terms must state: promoter name + address (doc 01) · entry period with close date/time · eligibility (18+/UK unless stated) · prize description and number · winner-selection method (random draw, verifiably fair, independent or auditable) · winner announcement date + how contacted · publicity extent (winner's surname/county unless they object) · platform disclaimer (Instagram/TikTok not sponsors). Prizes awarded **as described, on time — no substitutions downward, no rollovers to dodge awarding**.

**Tax:** giveaway prizes = marketing expense (keep invoices + register). Personal/business **gifts** are logged separately — gifts over £50/person/year or food/drink/tobacco/vouchers are generally not deductible (doc 06 §4). Influencer "gifting" in exchange for posts = payment in kind — must be declared as #ad by the recipient and logged.

## 4. Data protection
Entrant data per doc 11: consent wording at entry, entrants deleted ~6 months post-draw, winner records kept 6 years for audit.
