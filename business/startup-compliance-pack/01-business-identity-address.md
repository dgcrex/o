## 2. Address strategy (three distinct addresses — do not mix them up)

| Address type | Purpose | Recommended option |
|---|---|---|
| Registered office | Companies House public record + statutory mail | Paid registered-office service (keeps home address private) |
| Director service address | Director's public correspondence address | Same provider as registered office |
| Trading/mailbox address | Customer returns, supplier mail, bank statements | Virtual mailbox with scanning + forwarding |

**Legal requirements the address service must meet:**
- [ ] A real, physical UK address in the same UK jurisdiction as incorporation (England & Wales / Scotland / NI) — PO Box numbers alone are not acceptable for a registered office.
- [ ] Provider forwards or scans statutory mail (Companies House, HMRC) — an "appropriate address" under the Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act: mail must reach the company and delivery must be capable of acknowledgement.
- [ ] Provider is itself supervised for AML (they will ID-check you — have passport + proof of address ready).

## 4. Consistency rule (submission-grade standard)
