# 09 — HEALTH & SAFETY

**Status:** DRAFT · **Risk:** LOW–MEDIUM (scales with staff/premises) · **Approval:** L3

## 1. What applies at each stage

| Stage | Duty |
|---|---|
| Solo director, home-based | General duty of care; home workstation self-assessment; safe stock storage |
| Under 5 employees | Risk assessments required, but written policy not mandatory — write one anyway (audit-grade) |
| 5+ employees | Written H&S policy + written risk assessments legally required |
| Any employees at all | Employers' liability insurance (£5m minimum) legally required — display certificate |
| Photo/video shoots with models | Risk assessment per shoot/location (see doc 12 link) |
| Public events/pop-ups | Public liability insurance + venue risk assessment |

## 2. Baseline risk assessment (complete once, review annually or on change) — `ACTION REQUIRED`

Five steps per HSE method: identify hazards → who could be harmed → evaluate & control → record → review.

Starter hazard list for this business model:
- Manual handling of stock boxes (lifting technique, weight limits)
- Storage: stacking, fire load, blocked exits
- Electrical: chargers, lighting equipment for shoots — visual checks, no daisy-chaining
- Display screen equipment: workstation setup
- Shoot-specific: trip hazards from cables/equipment, hot lights, location hazards, lone working, late finishes and safe travel for models/crew
- Packaging tools: knives/cutters — retractable blades, first-aid kit stocked

## 3. Shoot safety protocol (attach to every model/artist booking — cross-ref doc 12)

1. Named person responsible for safety on the day.
2. Location risk assessment completed and saved before call time (doc 08 → /18-HS/).
3. Welfare basics: breaks, water, temperature, changing space with privacy, no lone situations without consent, chaperone policy for anyone under 18 (and licensing check — doc 10).
4. Incident log: anything reportable recorded same day; RIDDOR reporting if serious.
5. Insurance confirmed to cover the shoot type/location before booking.

## 4. Records
Risk assessments (versioned) · incident log (even if empty — dated "no incidents" reviews) · insurance certificates · equipment check log. All in /18-HS/.

## Approval route
- Writing assessments/policy: L1.
- Buying insurance, booking venues: **L3/L4 — HOLD.**
