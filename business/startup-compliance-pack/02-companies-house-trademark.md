# 02 — COMPANIES HOUSE INCORPORATION & TRADEMARK

**Status:** DRAFT · **Risk:** MEDIUM · **Approval:** L4 (official filings) — PREPARE + HOLD

> Note: Companies House registers the **company**; the UK Intellectual Property Office (IPO) registers the **trademark**. They are two separate filings — a company name is NOT trademark protection, and a trademark does not reserve a company name.

## 1. Pre-incorporation checks — `ACTION REQUIRED`

- [ ] Company name availability — Companies House name availability checker.
- [ ] Trademark conflict search — IPO trademark search (search identical AND similar marks in your classes) BEFORE committing to the name.
- [ ] Domain availability (.co.uk / .com) — buy at the same time as the name decision.
- [ ] Social handle availability — consistent handle across platforms.
- [ ] Name rules: no "sensitive words" (e.g. Royal, Bank, Charity) without permission; must end "Ltd"/"Limited".

## 4. Trademark registration (UK IPO)

1. **Classes:** identify Nice classes matching real activity — likely candidates: Class 25 (clothing) / Class 35 (retail services, advertising) / Class 41 (entertainment/artist services) — `PLACEHOLDER` until business scope confirmed.
2. **Search:** full IPO search for identical/similar marks in chosen classes. Record results (screenshot + date) into the audit file.
3. **File:** UK application (from £170 one class + £50/class extra, standard route; Right Start option splits the fee — verify current fees). Word mark vs logo mark: word mark gives broader protection; file logo separately if budget allows.
4. **Timeline:** ~2 weeks examination → 2-month opposition period → registration ≈ 4 months if unopposed.
5. **International:** UK registration covers UK only. EU (EUIPO) or Madrid Protocol filings are separate decisions for doc 15/16 scope — L4, later phase.
6. **Renewal:** every 10 years — set reminder.

**Warning:** ignore invoice-style letters from third-party "trademark registries" after filing — common scam; only IPO correspondence is real.
