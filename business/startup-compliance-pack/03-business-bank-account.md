# 03 — BUSINESS BANK ACCOUNT

**Status:** DRAFT · **Risk:** MEDIUM · **Approval:** L4 (finance application) — PREPARE + HOLD

## 1. Sequence

Incorporate first (doc 02) → then open the account in the **company's exact registered name**. A limited company legally requires its own account — never trade through a personal account (breaks the corporate veil, fails audit, blocks the startup loan).

**Declare international trade honestly at application** — undeclared international volume is a common cause of account freezes later.

## 3. Provider selection criteria (score 1–5 each, pick highest)

- [ ] Accepts your sector (some banks restrict e-commerce/imports/adult-adjacent modelling content — check appetite first to avoid a rejection on file).
- [ ] Multi-currency / international payments cost (SWIFT fees, FX margin).
- [ ] Integrations: accounting software feed (doc 06/08), payment processor payouts (doc 14).
- [ ] FSCS protection £85k (full-licence banks) vs e-money safeguarding (fintechs) — know which you're getting.
- [ ] Cash deposit ability if ever needed; dispute/chargeback support.

Shortlist to prepare for user decision: 2 fintech + 2 high-street options with fees table — L1 task, build when trading profile confirmed.

## 4. Account hygiene rules (audit-grade from day one)

1. All business income and expenses through this account only.
2. Director money in = documented director's loan; money out = salary/dividend/loan repayment, each documented (doc 06/08).
3. Connect the bank feed to accounting software in week one.
4. Separate savings "pot" for tax provision: transfer 19–25% of profit monthly (Corporation Tax) + VAT collected if registered.
5. Keep the startup loan (doc 04) in a traceable line — lenders and auditors will follow it.

## Approval route
- Building the provider comparison: L1.
- Submitting the account application / signing terms: **L4 — HOLD for user.**
