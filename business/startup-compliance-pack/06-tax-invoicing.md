## 1. Registrations timeline

| Registration | Trigger | Deadline | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporation Tax (HMRC) | Company starts trading | Within 3 months of trading | `ACTION REQUIRED` after doc 02 |
| PAYE | First salary paid (incl. director) | Before first payday | `ACTION REQUIRED` if salaried |
| VAT | Taxable turnover > £90,000 rolling 12 months (2024–25 threshold — verify current) OR voluntary | Within 30 days of breach | Monitor monthly |
| EORI number | First import/export | Before first shipment | Cross-ref doc 15 |

**VAT voluntary registration decision:** register early if B2B-heavy or reclaiming import VAT matters; stay under if B2C price-sensitive. Decision memo to prepare when turnover model exists — L1, then L5 confirm.

## 3. Compliant invoice template (fields — every invoice, no exceptions)

1. The word "Invoice" + unique sequential number (no gaps, no reuse)
2. Company name, registered number, registered office (doc 01 master copy)
3. Customer name & address
4. Invoice date (+ tax point if VAT-registered and different)
5. Description, quantity, unit price per line
6. Subtotal · VAT rate & amount per line (if registered) · Total
7. VAT number (if registered)
8. Payment terms (e.g. 14 days), bank details (doc 03), late-payment interest reservation (Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act — B2B)
9. International invoices: currency, Incoterm (doc 15), "reverse charge" wording where applicable

Store every issued invoice as PDF in the audit file (doc 08) — sequential folder by month.

## 4. Records HMRC requires you to keep (6 years)

Sales & purchase invoices · bank statements · import/export documents (15) · payroll records · VAT workings · asset purchases · director's loan account log · giveaway/gift cost records (13 — these are marketing expenses; keep winner records).

**Gifts note:** business gifts to any one person over £50/year, or food/drink/tobacco/vouchers, are generally not tax-deductible and may carry VAT consequences — log gifts separately so the accountant can treat them correctly. Prize-draw giveaways are marketing costs — keep terms + winner evidence per doc 13.
