# 12 — MODEL & ARTIST CONTRACTS (CONTRIBUTOR FRAMEWORK)

**Status:** DRAFT (clause frameworks — final wording needs L5 solicitor review before first signature) · **Risk:** HIGH (IP + people) · **Approval:** L4 sign-off per contract; L5 template review

## 1. Model release / booking agreement — required clauses

1. **Parties & date** — company (full registered details, doc 01) + model (or agency). `PLACEHOLDER`
2. **Engagement**: shoot date(s), location, call time, usage purpose.
3. **Fee & payment**: amount, payment within X days by bank transfer (never cash-off-books — audit trail doc 08), or documented TFP (time-for-prints) if genuinely unpaid — no "exposure" as sole consideration for commercial campaigns.
4. **Usage licence**: exactly which channels (web store, social, ads), territory (worldwide for online), duration (fixed term or perpetual — state it), whether image can be edited.
5. **Exclusions**: no sublicensing to third parties without consent; no use in sensitive contexts (political, adult, endorsement of unrelated products).
6. **Alias & privacy**: model's public credit name; personal data handled per doc 11.
7. **Withdrawal**: future-use stop rights vs already-published material.
8. **Safety & welfare**: shoot protocol (doc 09 §3) incorporated; right to refuse unsafe direction.
9. **Age verification**: photo ID checked and recorded for EVERY model — under-18s require parental consent + child performance licence check (doc 10) and are out of scope until explicitly approved (L5).
10. **Status**: independent contractor, responsible for own tax — plus IR35/employment-status note for repeat engagements (L5 check if regular).

## 2. Artist agreement (artwork on products / commissions) — required clauses

1. **Deliverables**: artwork spec, format, deadline, revisions included.
2. **IP structure — choose per deal, never leave silent:**
   - **Licence** (artist keeps copyright; company gets exclusive/non-exclusive right to reproduce on defined products, territory, term, royalty or flat fee); or
   - **Assignment** (company buys copyright outright — must be in writing and signed to be effective).
3. **Moral rights**: credit requirements (attribution on product page) or written waiver.
4. **Originality warranty**: artist confirms work is original, no third-party IP (fonts, sampled imagery, AI-training disputes), indemnity for breach.
5. **Payment**: flat fee / royalty % of net sales with quarterly statements — define "net" precisely.
6. **Merchandising rights**: which product types; new product types = new consent or pre-agreed list.
7. **Termination & sell-off**: existing stock may be sold for X months after termination.
8. **Contacts register**: artist name, alias, contact, portfolio link, agreement date, terms summary → /12-CONTRACTS/ index (doc 08).

## 3. Signing & storage discipline

- Signed PDF (e-signature acceptable) BEFORE shoot/production — no signature, no shoot, no print.
- Counter-signed copy sent to the contributor.
- Register row per contract: name · type · signed date · term end · usage scope · fee. Review quarterly for expiring usage rights (using an image after the licence lapses = infringement claim).

## 4. Payments to contributors

Bank transfer with invoice from contributor (or self-billing invoice you issue — agree in contract). International contributors: currency, who bears fees, and withholding-tax check per country — **L5 accountant** before first international payment. Cross-ref doc 14.
