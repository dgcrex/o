# 07 — SOURCING & SUPPLIER VERIFICATION

**Status:** DRAFT · **Risk:** MEDIUM · **Approval:** L3 (supplier contact/orders) — PREPARE + HOLD

## 1. Supplier due-diligence file (one per supplier — before first order)

| Check | Evidence to store | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Legal identity | Company registration extract (Companies House / local registry / China: business licence via verification service) | `ACTION REQUIRED` |
| Real trading history | Years trading, references, platform transaction history | `ACTION REQUIRED` |
| Product compliance capability | Test reports (CE/UKCA where applicable), material certs, safety data | `ACTION REQUIRED` |
| Sample quality | Ordered sample + dated photo record + notes | `ACTION REQUIRED` |
| Payment terms & method | Pro-forma invoice; NEVER pay personal accounts; use platform escrow/trade assurance where possible | Rule |
| Ethical baseline | No forced-labour red flags; supplier code of conduct signed | Template below |
| IP safety | Product doesn't infringe third-party trademarks/designs (counterfeits = seizure at customs + liability) | `ACTION REQUIRED` |

Verdict per supplier: APPROVED / CONDITIONAL / REJECTED — recorded with date and reviewer.

## 2. Product compliance by category (gate before listing anything)

- General products: General Product Safety Regulations — safe, traceable, labelled with your business identity.
- UKCA/CE marking needed for: electronics, toys, PPE, cosmetics-adjacent — check category before ordering. `PLACEHOLDER` until product list confirmed.
- Textiles: fibre-content labelling; nickel limits on jewellery; flammability rules for nightwear/furniture.
- **As the importer, YOU are legally the responsible person for product safety in the UK market** — supplier promises don't transfer the duty (cross-ref doc 15).

## 3. Purchase-order discipline

1. Every order on a written PO: spec, quantity, price, Incoterm (15), delivery date, defect/return terms.
2. Photograph goods on arrival; log defects same day; claim window noted.
3. Keep supplier invoices + payment proof matched to POs (audit trail, doc 08; customs valuation evidence, doc 15).

## 4. Supplier code of conduct (one-pager to send with first PO) — template points

No child/forced labour · safe working conditions · lawful materials · no subcontracting without notice · right to request compliance evidence · truthful documentation. Signature + date.

## Approval route
- Building due-diligence files and templates: L1.
- Contacting suppliers, placing sample orders, payments: **L3 — HOLD** (payments L4).
