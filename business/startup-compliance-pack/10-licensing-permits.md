# 10 — LICENSING & PERMITS (DECISION TREE)

**Status:** DRAFT · **Risk:** MEDIUM (gates depend on final product scope — `PLACEHOLDER` until confirmed) · **Approval:** L4

> Compliance gate rule: for any regulated activity, the licence comes FIRST — before sourcing, listing, or advertising. If a planned activity is unlawful without a licence, the plan routes to the fastest lawful path, not around it.

## 1. Decision tree — answer YES/NO for the final business scope (`ACTION REQUIRED`)

## 2. Music licensing note (shoots + content)
Commercial content (ads, promo videos, store playlists) needs licensed or royalty-free/commissioned music — platform "trending audio" licences generally do NOT cover business accounts. Cheapest lawful route: royalty-free subscription or commission artists directly under doc 12 terms.

## 3. Record
For each YES: licence body · application date · reference · expiry/renewal · certificate PDF in /BUSINESS-MASTER/ per doc 08. Renewal reminders set.
