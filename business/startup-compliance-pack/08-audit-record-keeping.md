# 08 — AUDIT & RECORD-KEEPING FRAMEWORK (SUBMISSION-GRADE FILING)

**Status:** DRAFT (framework ready to adopt) · **Risk:** LOW · **Approval:** L0/L1 — internal

> This is the organisation standard the whole pack promises: every document findable in under a minute, labelled, dated, and consistent — the same standard you'd need for any formal submission or inspection.

## 1. Master folder structure (mirror in cloud drive + this repo)

```
/BUSINESS-MASTER/
  01-COMPANY/        incorporation cert, articles, statutory registers, CS01s
  02-ADDRESSES/      address service contracts, renewal proof
  03-BANK/           account docs, statements by month (YYYY-MM)
  04-FINANCE/        loan application, agreements, business plan versions
  05-TAX/            HMRC letters, VAT returns, CT600, PAYE
  06-INVOICES-OUT/   YYYY/MM, sequential
  07-INVOICES-IN/    supplier invoices matched to POs
  08-SUPPLIERS/      one folder per supplier: due diligence + POs + comms
  09-PRODUCTS/       specs, test reports, UKCA/CE evidence, labels
  10-WEBSITE/        legal pages (versioned), test-purchase evidence
  11-DATA-PROTECTION/ ICO cert, policies, DPIA, breach log, SAR log
  12-CONTRACTS/      model releases, artist agreements, signed PDFs only
  13-GIVEAWAYS/      per promotion: terms, entries proof, winner record
  14-PAYMENTS/       PSP agreements, payout reports, chargeback files
  15-IMPORT-EXPORT/  per shipment: invoice, packing list, customs entry, duty proof
  16-TRAVEL-VISA/    per trip: purpose memo, invitations, visa docs
  17-INSURANCE/      policies + schedules
  18-HS/             risk assessments, incident log
  99-CORRESPONDENCE/ official letters by sender, dated
```

## 2. Naming convention (no exceptions)

`YYYY-MM-DD_source_doctype_ref.pdf` — e.g. `2026-08-01_HMRC_CT-registration_UTR.pdf`. Scans: PDF, readable, complete (all pages). Originals of statutory documents kept physically in one labelled folder.

## 3. Retention schedule

| Records | Keep |
|---|---|
| Company statutory records | Life of company |
| Accounting/tax (HMRC) | 6 years minimum |
| VAT & customs/import evidence | 6 years (customs 4+ but keep 6) |
| Contracts (models/artists) | 6 years after contract ends |
| Personal data | Only per retention policy (doc 11) — don't hoard |
| H&S incident records | 3 years minimum (longer if injury claim possible) |

## 4. Internal audit loop (monthly, 30 minutes)

1. Bank reconciled in accounting software — zero unexplained transactions.
2. Invoice sequence unbroken; all filed.
3. New suppliers have complete due-diligence files (07).
4. Gap table (17) updated — labels upgraded where evidence arrived.
5. Upcoming deadlines (06 calendar) reviewed — nothing inside 30 days unprepared.
6. One-line audit log entry: date · checks done · issues · fixes. PASS / PASS-WITH-GAPS / FAIL.

## 5. Submission-ready formatting standard (any formal bundle)

Indexed front page → numbered documents → paginated (`Page X of Y`) → chronological within sections → every fact labelled (`VERIFIED`/etc.) → no document referenced but missing. A bundle that fails any of these does not go out.

L0 — adopt immediately; filing and logging are internal actions.
