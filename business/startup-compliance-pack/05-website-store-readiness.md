## 2. Legally required pages & notices (UK e-commerce) — every box before launch

- [ ] **Company details on site** (Companies Act + E-Commerce Regs): full registered name, company number, registered office, contact email — in footer and/or contact page.
- [ ] **Terms & conditions of sale** — order process, when contract forms, delivery, prices incl. taxes.
- [ ] **Returns/cancellation policy** (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013): 14-day cancellation right for distance sales, refund within 14 days of return; state exceptions (personalised goods, hygiene-sealed items). Failing to inform extends cancellation to 12 months.
- [ ] **Consumer Rights Act 2015** compliance: goods must be as described, satisfactory quality, fit for purpose — 30-day short-term right to reject.
- [ ] **Privacy policy** (doc 11) + **cookie banner** with genuine consent (PECR) — non-essential cookies off until opted in.
- [ ] **Prices** shown inclusive of VAT (if registered) and with delivery costs disclosed before checkout — no hidden fees (DMCCA 2025 pricing transparency).
- [ ] **Giveaway/competition terms** page (doc 13).
- [ ] **Accessibility** basics: alt text, contrast, keyboard navigation (WCAG 2.1 AA target).

## 3. Order section / checkout requirements

1. Order confirmation email with full T&Cs attached or linked (durable medium requirement).
2. "Order with obligation to pay" — the pay button must say so clearly (e.g. "Pay now").
3. Invoice/receipt generation wired to accounting (doc 06 invoice fields).
4. Shipping matrix: zones, costs, duties responsibility for international (cross-ref doc 15 — say who pays import duty; DDP vs DAP).
5. Stock sync so you cannot sell what you cannot source (doc 07).
6. Payment methods live and tested in sandbox (doc 14).
7. Fraud rules on (address/CVV mismatch holds).
